Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how 1C Reports VM uses Google user data.

Google data accessed

The application uses read-only Gmail access to identify messages containing business report attachments and to retrieve those attachments.

How data is used

Retrieved report files are used only for internal business reporting and automation. Report files may be stored in the organization's reporting folders and delivered to authorized internal Telegram chats.

Data modification

The application does not send, modify, or delete Gmail messages.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold. Report information is used only for the internal reporting workflow and is shared only with authorized internal recipients and services required to operate that workflow.

Data retention

Report files may be retained as part of the organization's internal reporting archive.

Contact

Questions about this application and its use of Google user data may be sent to the support email shown on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
